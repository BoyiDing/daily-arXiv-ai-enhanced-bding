Abstract: We present a comprehensive analysis of the radial dark-matter (DM) density profiles of cosmic filaments in the hydrodynamical simulation TNG50. We show that the filament spine locations returned directly by DisPerSE do not accurately reflect the true density ridges. Finally, we show that the universal filament profile is accurately described by a generalized triple-power-law model.

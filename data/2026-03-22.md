*Zachary J. Weiner*

Main category: astro-ph.CO

Dynamical dark energy models explain the excess through unphysical high-redshift extrapolations. Abstract: We present a simplified and general description of the high-redshift information in acoustic scale measurements from the cosmic microwave background and large-scale structure.

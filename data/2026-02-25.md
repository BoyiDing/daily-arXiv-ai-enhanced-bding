### [1] [Dynamical friction on binary stars in dark matter dominated environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20245)
*Nicolas Esser*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: Binary stars in dark matter environments experience dynamical friction that causes eccentricity oscillations and semi-major axis decay, potentially biasing velocity dispersion measurements in dwarf galaxies.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To understand how binary stars evolve in dark matter-dominated environments and whether they can be used as probes of dark matter content in faint galaxies.

Method: Derived formulas and timescales for secular evolution of orbital parameters for wide and close binaries moving through uniform dark matter background with center-of-mass motion considered.

Result: Binaries in ultra-faint dwarf galaxies undergo significant eccentricity oscillations while semi-major axes decrease gradually; dynamical friction can enhance bias from unresolved binaries in velocity dispersion measurements.

Conclusion: Binary stars may serve as a tool to probe dark matter content in faint galaxies with future theoretical studies and improved observational capabilities.

Abstract: We study binary stars moving through a uniform dark matter background and experiencing dynamical friction. The centre-of-mass motion of the pairs is taken into account. We derive formulas and timescales for the secular evolution of the orbital parameters for both wide and close binaries. We apply these results to environments typical of dark matter dominated ultra-faint dwarf galaxies and show that some binaries undergo significant eccentricity oscillations, while their semi-major axes decrease more gradually. We consider a simple binary star population and find that dynamical friction, notably, can enhance the bias from unresolved binaries in velocity dispersion measurements. With future, more detailed theoretical studies and improving observational capabilities, binary stars may serve as a tool to probe the dark matter content of some of the faintest galaxies.
